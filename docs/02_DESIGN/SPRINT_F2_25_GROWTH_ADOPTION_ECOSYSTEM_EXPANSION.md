# 1. Mission

Define how GMRLOG grows.

Growth exists to expand **gaming culture**.

Not to maximize user addiction.

Growth should feel like:

> “A thriving ecosystem naturally attracting more people.”

Never:

> “A machine optimizing for signups.”

Align North Star: a larger home for gaming culture — not a denser funnel.

---

# 2. Growth Philosophy

| Law |
|-----|
| Growth serves culture |
| Culture never serves growth |

| Always expand by | Never expand by |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Belonging worth sharing | Compulsion |
| Craft worth reading | Viral dark patterns |
| Communities worth joining | Fake scarcity |
| Discovery worth exploring | Engagement farming |
| Trust worth recommending | Invitation pressure |

Browse-first / guest dignity (F2.2) remains: curiosity may precede signup — signup is not a hostage gate for culture reading where freezes allow.

Kinship F2.22: DAU-at-all-costs and infinite retention are illegitimate growth laws.

---

# 3. Organic Growth

Organic growth is the primary constitutional path.

| Channel |
|---------|
| Player invitations |
| Creator invitations |
| Community invitations |
| Game discovery |
| Word-of-mouth |

## Rules

| Rule |
|------|
| Invitations are optional · never nagging loops |
| Sharing is intentional — never forced |
| Word-of-mouth follows pride in Digital Home — not rewards for spam |
| Auth / onboarding remain identity discovery — not growth interrogation (F2.2.1) |
| Premium never buys viral privilege that creates two communities (F2.16) |

Organic does not mean “unmeasured forever.”

It means measurement never becomes manipulation (F2.23 · F2.22).

---

# 4. Ecosystem Expansion

How new groups enter GMRLOG:

| Group |
|-------|
| Players |
| Developers |
| Studios |
| Publishers |
| Board Gamers |
| Cosplayers |
| Universities |
| Gaming Clubs |

## Rules

| Rule |
|------|
| Entry expands culture graph — does not invent new product protagonists |
| Organizations enter as contributors (F2.24) — never owners |
| External guests remain optional bridges (F2.21) — never foundations |
| Accessibility & global dignity apply to every entrant (F2.18) |
| Six pillars remain equal — expansion must not crown one pillar as growth engine |

New audiences inherit the same constitution.

They do not receive a diluted or corporate-skewed GMRLOG.

---

# 5. Regional Expansion

**Global-first** philosophy (F2.18).

| Always | Never |
|--------|-------|
| Many perspectives · one culture | One-country-first culture |
| Localized access without fragmentation | Separate regional products that split belonging |
| Regional events as culture moments (F2.15) | Regional monoculture as default player |
| Multilingual hospitality reserved | Language as caste |

No country becomes the “default player.”

Country launch programs, if reserved later, remain adoption posture — not identity rewrite.

---

# 6. Partnership Philosophy

Partners strengthen culture.

Never own it (F2.24 · F2.21).

| Partners may | Partners must never |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Amplify games · events · education · creators | Buy discovery ranking |
| Official presence with transparency | Hidden advertising as organic growth |
| Campus / local / cultural bridges | Force engagement or fake orgs |
| Align Trust verification | Purchase reputation or Community Trust |

Partnership is hospitality.

Not acquisition outsourcing that sells the home.

---

# 7. Community Expansion

Communities grow **themselves** (F2.11).

| Healthy | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Belonging · topical gravity · guides | Artificial inflation |
| Organic invites from members | Bot members · fake activity |
| Autonomy under platform constitution | Growth quotas as community law |

Community Trust remains unbuyable.

Guilds ≠ Communities — expansion must not blur that freeze.

---

# 8. Creator Expansion

Creators attract creators (F2.12).

| Healthy | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Craft gravity · series · guides · articles | Creator factories |
| Vertical growth on Profile | Clout farms as onboarding |
| Free reading of culture | Paywalled “creator growth packs” that buy reach |

Creator Economy enhances — never turns expansion into content mills or AI fake personalities (F2.19).

---

# 9. Discover Growth

Better discovery.

Not more engagement (F2.10 · F2.19).

| Growth via Discover means | Never means |
|---------------------------|-------------|
| Taste-first paths that help newcomers find home | Infinite compulsive streams |
| Explainable suggestions | Black-box viral ranking |
| Hidden gems & culture breadth | Sold placement (F2.16) |
| Interruptible exploration | Doom-scroll as acquisition |

Discover grows the ecosystem by **orientation**.

Not by retention theater.

---

# 10. Relationship Graph

Growth **expands** participation across the graph.

It never changes graph philosophy.

```
Players
  ↓
Games
  ↓
Communities
  ↓
Creators
  ↓
Events
  ↓
Organizations
```

| Growth may | Growth must not |
|------------|-----------------|
| Add more authentic nodes & edges | Redefine pillars or protagonist |
| Widen culture hospitality | Convert edges into spam vectors |
| Honor Trust · Agency · Accessibility | Override prior freezes for acquisition |

Identity remains the player’s (F2.5.1).

Growth is more people finding that home — not redesigning the house into a funnel.

---

# 11. Anti-Manipulation

Explicit bans:

| Ban |
|-----|
| Referral spam |
| Fake invites |
| Growth hacks |
| Viral dark patterns |
| Forced sharing |
| Fake scarcity |
| Engagement farming |
| Invitation pressure |
| Fake communities / fake activity for charts (F2.17 · F2.22) |
| Purchased visibility as “growth” (F2.16 · F2.24) |
| FOMO analytics as acquisition law (F2.23) |

If growth requires the player to feel hunted, the tactic is illegitimate.

Reserve architecture only:

| Campus Program |
| Community Leaders |
| Local Events |

Architecture only.

Ambassadors / leaders, if ever, remain cultural stewardship — never paid influence markets or unbuyable-role bypasses (F2.11 · F2.16 · F2.24).

---

# 13. Emotional Goal

Growth should feel like:

> “This community naturally keeps growing.”

Never:

> “I am constantly being asked to invite people.”

And never:

> “Belonging here depends on recruiting.”
