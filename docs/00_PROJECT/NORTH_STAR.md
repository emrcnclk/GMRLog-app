# Why GMRLOG Exists

GMRLOG exists to become the digital home of gaming culture.

Not just a game tracker.

Not just a review website.

Not just another social network.

A place where everyone who loves gaming can belong.

---

# Vision

To build the world's most complete social ecosystem for gaming culture.

Players.

Board Gamers.

Developers.

Studios.

Content Creators.

Communities.

All connected in one platform.

---

# Mission

Help people discover games.

Help people share experiences.

Help people build communities.

Help developers build better games.

Create meaningful connections through gaming.

---

# Product Identity

GMRLOG is not competing with Steam.

GMRLOG is not competing with Discord.

GMRLOG is not competing with Letterboxd.

GMRLOG is not competing with Reddit.

GMRLOG combines the best parts of all of them into one ecosystem designed specifically for gaming culture.

---

# Gaming Culture

Gaming culture includes, but is not limited to:

- Video Games
- Board Games
- Tabletop RPGs
- Gaming Communities
- Game Music (OST)
- Game Adaptation Movies & TV Shows
- Indie Games
- Mods
- Esports
- Game Development
- Game Jams
- Fan Art
- Cosplay
- Hardware
- Streaming
- Gaming Events

If it belongs to gaming culture, it belongs on GMRLOG.

# Product Principles

## Gaming First

Everything starts with gaming.

Every feature should strengthen the gaming ecosystem.

Never become a generic social network.

---

## Digital Home

Users should feel:

"This is my place."

Profiles should express identity.

Communities should create belonging.

Friends should feel close.

---

## Community Before Monetization

People come first.

Communities come first.

Trust comes first.

Revenue is a consequence of creating value.

Never sacrifice user experience for short-term monetization.

---

## Premium Experience

Every interaction should feel polished.

Every screen should feel intentional.

Quality matters more than quantity.

Small details create memorable experiences.

---

## AI Native

Artificial Intelligence is part of the platform.

Not an add-on.

AI should improve:

- Discovery
- Search
- Feed
- Recommendations
- Moderation
- Personalization
- Developer Insights

without replacing human interaction.

---

## Build for Decades

Choose architecture over shortcuts.

Choose maintainability over speed.

Choose scalability over hacks.

Every decision should still make sense years from now.

---

# Player Identity

Every player should have a home.

Profiles should become living gaming identities.

Players should proudly showcase:

- Reviews
- Lists
- Collections
- Tier Lists
- Achievements
- Badges
- Favorite Games
- Statistics
- Memories

Profiles should feel personal.

Not generic.

---

# Developers

Developers are first-class citizens.

GMRLOG should help developers:

- Build communities
- Receive feedback
- Share updates
- Publish demos
- Reach players
- Understand audiences

without replacing existing storefronts.

---

# Studios

Studios should be able to:

- Advertise responsibly
- Engage communities
- Launch campaigns
- Organize events

Advertising should always respect the player experience.

---

# Monetization

Revenue should come from helping the ecosystem grow.

Examples include:

- Premium Membership
- Sponsored Content
- Developer Services
- Studio Advertising
- Marketplace Fees
- Event Sponsorships
- Analytics Products

Players should never feel like the product.

---

# Long-Term Vision

GMRLOG should become:

The place where people:

discover games,

talk about games,

review games,

collect games,

share experiences,

meet friends,

join communities,

support developers,

play browser games,

participate in game jams,

build careers,

and create memories.

---

# The North Star Question

Every feature must answer one question:

"Does this make GMRLOG a better digital home for gaming culture?"

If the answer is YES:

Build it.

If the answer is NO:

Do not build it.
