# Product Vision

## Purpose

This document defines the long-term vision of GMRLOG.

Unlike the Project Charter, which establishes business and engineering boundaries, this document explains **what GMRLOG should become** over the next decade.

Every product decision should move the platform closer to this vision.

---

# Vision Statement

> **To become the world's definitive digital home for gamers, where every game played, every memory created, and every opinion shared becomes part of a player's lifelong gaming identity.**

---

# Mission

GMRLOG empowers players to preserve their gaming journey while helping developers build meaningful relationships with their communities.

Gaming is more than entertainment.

It is culture.

It is identity.

It is memory.

GMRLOG exists to preserve all three.

---

# Why GMRLOG Exists

Today's gaming ecosystem is fragmented.

A player may use:

* Steam to buy games.
* Discord to communicate.
* Reddit to ask questions.
* YouTube to watch reviews.
* Twitch to watch gameplay.
* X (Twitter) for announcements.
* Notes to track completed games.
* Spreadsheets to manage backlogs.
* Multiple websites to rate games.

No single platform captures the entire gaming experience.

GMRLOG is designed to become that platform.

---

# Our Long-Term Goal

When someone finishes a game, their first instinct should be:

> "I'm going to log this on GMRLOG."

When someone hears about a new release:

> "Let's see what GMRLOG thinks."

When someone wants recommendations:

> "My GMRLOG feed probably has something."

When someone asks:

> "What games define you?"

The answer should simply be:

> "Check my GMRLOG profile."

# Product Values

## Authenticity

Encourage honest opinions instead of engagement farming.

Reviews should reward depth rather than controversy.

---

## Discovery

The platform should constantly introduce players to experiences they would never have discovered otherwise.

Discovery should feel personal, not algorithmically forced.

---

## Community

Healthy discussion is more valuable than viral content.

Communities should celebrate games rather than compete for attention.

---

## Creativity

Users should have many ways to express themselves.

Examples include:

* Reviews
* Lists
* Tier Lists
* Diaries
* Collections
* Long-form articles
* Screenshots
* Clips

---

## Longevity

Every profile should grow richer over time.

The value of a GMRLOG account should increase every year.

Deleting years of memories should feel unimaginable.

# Premium Experience Philosophy

Premium does not mean adding unnecessary visual effects.

Premium means:

* Fast interactions.
* Elegant typography.
* Meaningful animations.
* Thoughtful spacing.
* Excellent accessibility.
* Beautiful transitions.
* Consistent behavior.

Every screen should communicate craftsmanship.

---

# Community Philosophy

Communities should revolve around shared passion.

Not outrage.

Not controversy.

Not engagement bait.

Healthy communities naturally create long-term retention.

---

# Developer Philosophy

Developers are first-class citizens.

Verified developers should be able to:

* Publish updates.
* Respond to reviews.
* Share development blogs.
* Announce events.
* Organize betas.
* Interact directly with players.

The relationship between players and developers should feel collaborative.

---

# AI Vision

Artificial Intelligence should assist—not replace—the community.

AI responsibilities include:

* Personalized recommendations.
* Duplicate review detection.
* Toxicity moderation assistance.
* Spam prevention.
* Intelligent search.
* Review summaries.
* Accessibility improvements.

AI should never fabricate community opinions.
