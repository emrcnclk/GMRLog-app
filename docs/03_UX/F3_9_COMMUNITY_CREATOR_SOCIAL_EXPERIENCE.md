# 1. Mission

Define how community, creator, and social surfaces feel when humans meet around games.

Social graph supports the player’s identity.

It never consumes it.

Discussions orbit **games**.

Never egos.

Align Digital Home (F3.7) · Trust (F2.17) · gaming as common ground (F2.8).

---

# 2. Community Experience Philosophy

Communities are culture hubs (F2.11) — **never a Discord alternative**.

| Community should feel | Must never feel |
|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Belonging around games / topics | Always-on chat addiction OS |
| Local culture under platform constitution | Separate product with its own gravity well |
| Discoverable without pressure (F3.8 kinship) | Forced join for belonging |
| Autonomous yet Trust-bound | Brand channel first (F2.24) |

Guilds ≠ Communities — interaction feel must preserve that distinction (F2.11).

Community discovery feel: taste and topic — not growth spam.

---

# 3. Creator Experience Philosophy

Creators publish craft (F2.12) — **never an influencer economy**.

| Creator should feel | Must never feel |
|---------------------|-----------------|
| Vertical growth on Profile | Clout career ladder as UX |
| Long-form first-class reading | Engagement mill |
| Human byline · craft before automation | Fake personality theater (F2.19) |
| Tools that deepen expression | Metrics as identity |

Creator profile experience: same Digital Home family — creator section deepens story, does not replace home (F3.7 · F2.5.1).

---

# 4. Social Interaction Philosophy

| Always | Never |
|--------|-------|
| Gaming protagonist | Social-graph protagonist |
| Respect · agency · calm (F3.1 · F2.20) | Performative attention competition |
| Mute / block / report reachable (F2.8 · F2.17) | Dark-pattern re-enable |
| Reactions as light acknowledgment | Like-count identity |

Likes, follower counts, and engagement metrics must **never** be the product’s primary focus.

Toxicity is reduced; expression of disagreement about games remains welcome (F2.17 moderation philosophy).

No social interaction should produce “come back constantly” compulsion (F2.9 · F2.22).

---

# 5. Conversation Experience

Conversations (including DMs) follow F2.8: gaming-centered · not Discord replacement.

| Conversation should | Must never |
|---------------------|------------|
| Feel optional · interruptible | Become the home screen of the product |
| Preserve spoiler respect | Treat spoilers as engagement bait |
| Support clarity · recovery on errors (F3.6) | Shame or trap in threads |
| Allow exit without social punishment | Infinite unread FOMO theater |

Messages remain Profile/deep-link — not a tab (F2.1 · F3.2).

---

# 6. Review & Discussion Experience

Reviews and discussions are culture — **not Reddit fight clubs** · **not X/Twitter pile-ons**.

| Experience should | Must never |
|-------------------|------------|
| Orbit the game / work | Orbit personal status |
| Reward thoughtful reading (F3.1 · F3.8) | Optimize for quote-tweet combat energy |
| Keep comments secondary to authored work when reading | Let comment wars own the page |
| Allow disagreement without harassment UX | Amplify outrage modules |

Review interaction: acknowledge · reply · share sparingly — never metric theater as the point.

---

# 7. Follow / Friend / Guild Experience

| System | Must feel like | Must never feel like |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Follow | Taste / creator / community interest | Instagram follow economy |
| Friend | Gaming kinship / trust | Facebook social graph pressure |
| Guild | Structured play/belonging distinct from Communities | Discord server checklist |

| Shared laws |
|-------------|
| Reciprocity never forced for belonging |
| Counts never crown the UI |
| Discovery of people remains taste-adjacent — not clout charts |
| Unfollow / leave / mute without guilt (F3.6 empty/leave dignity) |

---

# 8. Community Participation

Participation is intentional contribution (F2.11).

| Participation may | Must never |
|-------------------|------------|
| Post · discuss · guide · host within rules | Artificial inflation of activity |
| Earn Community Trust through contribution — unbuyable (F2.16) | Pay-to-lead UX |
| Feel calm to join and calm to leave | Hostage membership loops |

Community participation never requires constant presence to “keep rank.”

---

# 9. Creator Relationship Experience

Players relate to creators as craft sources — not idols to farm.

| Relationship may | Must never |
|------------------|------------|
| Follow · read · discuss · support within F2.12 / F2.26 ethics | Convert readers into engagement labor |
| Collaborate feel: series · communities · events | Replace creators with org content mills (F2.24) |
| Protect creators from harassment UX paths (F2.17) | Remove protection when popular |

Collaboration feel: shared culture work — not growth hack co-marketing theater.

---

# 10. Reputation Visibility

Reputation visibility obeys F2.13: qualitative Known For · no XP/karma/leaderboards.

| Visibility should | Must never |
|-------------------|------------|
| Appear calm · sparse · contextual | Become a status scoreboard in social rooms |
| Support trust in craft / community care | Drive attention competition |
| Stay unbuyable (F2.16) | Sell badges as social rank |

Social surfaces may show recognition.

They may not become reputation casinos.

---

# 11. Event Participation Experience

Events are real culture moments (F2.15) — not battle-pass social seasons.

| Event participation should feel | Must never feel |
|---------------------------------|-----------------|
| Optional gathering around games | Mandatory FOMO calendar |
| Easy entry/exit | Streak attendance pressure |
| Compatible with communities/creators | Corporate takeover of Home (F3.8 · F2.24) |

Sharing event presence: intentional — never forced (F2.25).

---

# 12. Cross-Social Continuity

| Continuity |
|------------|
| Game Detail → Community / Creator doors (F3.8) remain secondary to relationship |
| Profile Creator vertical ↔ published work ↔ discussion without leaving Digital Home metaphor |
| Home may surface social culture sparsely — never becomes a social inbox |
| Notifications social category calm (F2.9) — never dictate return loops |
| Back restores orientation (F3.2) after community/creator deep entry |

Social graph edges serve identity and games.

They do not rewrite room jobs (Home / Discover / Library / Profile).

---

# 13. Accessibility

Social/community/creator UX must remain inclusive (F2.18 · F3.1–F3.6).

| Implication |
|-------------|
| Conversation and discussion readable · not motion-dependent |
| Critical safety actions (mute/block/report) not gesture-only |
| Reputation/recognition not color-only |
| Long-form creator reading first-class |
| Cognitive calm: no urgency social chrome |

Toxicity reduction UX must not erase accessible participation.

---

# 14. Consistency Rules

| Rule |
|------|
| Community ≠ Discord · Creator ≠ influencer OS · Follow ≠ Instagram · Friend ≠ Facebook · Review/Discussion ≠ Reddit/X |
| Metrics never primary focus on any social surface |
| Games remain common ground of talk |
| Guild vs Community distinction preserved in feel |
| Creator remains Profile-vertical · not a sixth tab |
| Sharing intentional · labeled when commercial (F2.26) |
| Trust tools consistently available |

---

# 15. Anti-Manipulation

Explicit bans:

| Ban |
|-----|
| Discord-alternative home feeling |
| Influencer-economy creator UX |
| Instagram-style follow pressure |
| Facebook-style friend pressure |
| Reddit/X combat discussion UX |
| Metric-first social layouts |
| Attention competition as product goal |
| “Come back constantly” social loops |
| Fake engagement / brigading affordances as features |
| Bought community authority / reputation visibility (F2.16 · F2.13) |
| Outrage amplification modules |
| Forced sharing / invite pressure in social rooms (F2.25) |

If a social pattern’s best argument is DAU or clout, it is illegitimate.

---

# 16. Future Ready

Reserve architecture only:

| Capability |
|------------|
| Richer community discovery without clout charts |
| Calmer collaboration between creators & communities |
| Event participation modes that stay optional |
| Reputation presentation refinements still qualitative |
| Safer conversation tools under F2.17 — still non-black-box |

No UI · no moderation algorithms · no implementation.

---

# 17. Emotional Goal

Social presence should feel like:

> “I enjoy being around other gamers.”

Never:

> “I have to perform for other gamers.”

Never:

> “I am competing for attention.”

---

# 19. MVP Final Integration Amendment — Anti-Manipulation Extension · Communities & Events

**Amendment:** MVP Final Integration Amendment (July 2026). §1–§18 experience philosophy is unchanged. This section **extends anti-manipulation rules** (§15) for MVP social surfaces.

## 19.1 Explicitly forbidden

| Forbidden |
|-----------|
| FOMO countdowns |
| Streak pressure |
| Achievement pressure |
| Engagement farming |
| Recommendation addiction loops |
| Artificial urgency |

These bans apply to Communities, Events, and every social surface that presents them.

## 19.2 Communities & Events — same ethical philosophy

Communities and Events must follow the same ethical philosophy as the rest of F3.9:

| Law |
|-----|
| Shared gaming culture over attention competition |
| Optional participation — never obligation |
| Metrics never primary |
| Games over egos |
| Calm over compulsion |

## 19.3 Community surfaces (feel)

| Surface | Experience rule |
|---------|-----------------|
| Community Detail | Feels like entering a room with a purpose — orientation before activity (§2) |
| Community Feed | Talk orbits games, not egos (§4 · §6) · density never becomes chat noise |
| Community Members | People, not follower counts (§7 · §10) |
| Community Activity | A calm record of what happened — never an engagement scoreboard (§8) |

| Law |
|-----|
| Joining is a deliberate, welcoming act; leaving is quiet and unpunished (§8) |
| Membership size and activity volume are never status pressure (§10) |
| Communities stay GMRLOG-native rooms — not a Discord surrogate (§2) |

## 19.4 Event participation (feel)

| Rule |
|------|
| Events feel like invitations to culture, never like seasons to grind (§11) |
| Participation is optional, reversible and pressure-free (§11 · §15) |
| No FOMO countdowns · no streaks · no attendance shaming |
| Community events feel like the room celebrating, not the platform demanding (§8 · §11) |
| Tournaments welcome competitive culture without turning identity into rank (§10) |

## 19.5 Discord boundary

| Rule |
|------|
| Discord may only be an identity convenience — it never appears as chat, presence or community structure here (F2.21 §20.2) |
| Conversation remains F2.8 · GMRLOG-owned and calm (§5) |
